Privacy Policy: Caller Info

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Caller Info ("the app") shows the Title, Department and Company of the person who is calling you, on your call screen, using the details already saved in your own phone contacts.

The short version

The app collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send any data anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Your contacts and call information never leave your phone.

Purpose of the app

When a call rings, Android shows only a name and number. Caller Info adds a small card with the caller's Title, Department and Company, taken from your contacts, so you know who is calling before you answer. The card closes by itself when you answer or the call ends.

Permissions, and why each one is needed

PermissionWhy the app needs it
Phone (READ_PHONE_STATE)To know when a call is ringing, answered or ended.
Call log (READ_CALL_LOG)Android gives an app the caller's number only if this permission is granted. The app uses it only to get the number of the call that is ringing. It does not read, list, save or send your call history.
Contacts (READ_CONTACTS)To find the caller in your contacts and read their name, Title, Department and Company. The app never changes your contacts.
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)To draw the information card on top of the incoming-call screen.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) and Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE)To show a small, silent "Caller Info is ready" notice. It keeps the app running so Android does not stop it and miss an incoming call. The notice does nothing else.
Run at start-up (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)To start that notice again after the phone restarts.
Ignore battery optimisation (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS)Optional. Lets the app ask you, with Android's own "Allow / Deny" prompt, to run without battery limits, so Android does not put it to sleep and miss a call. Nothing changes unless you tap Allow.

Before Android asks for the Phone, Call log and Contacts permissions, the app explains the reasons on screen. You can turn any permission off in Android Settings > Apps > Caller Info. Without them the card cannot be shown.

What is stored on your phone

Only your choices: the card position (Top, Middle or Bottom) and how the card looks (colours, "Match my phone", bold, text size, label language, hiding empty lines and the auto-hide timer). The "Match my phone" choice only reads your phone's own colour theme and light/dark setting, on the phone. The app keeps no call history, no copy of your contacts and no logs. Everything is removed when you uninstall the app.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer any data to anyone, because we do not collect any. No third-party code or services are used.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
